How to play

Say this out loud, quickly
  1. Split into teamsTwo to four teams. Each turn, one person from the team holds the phone and reads.
  2. Read it, don’t solve itThe reader says the words on the card out loud, fast, again and again. Don’t try to make sense of it. Let your mouth do the work.
  3. Teammates shout guessesEach card sounds like a well-known phrase, name or title. When someone says it, the reader taps Got it! and the card decodes itself.
  4. Keep the reader honestThe other teams can press and hold Peek to check the answer. Stuck? Skip (you get a limited number per turn).
  5. Time’s up? Steal itThe clock pauses while cards move. When it runs out, the card is revealed and another team can steal the point if they shouted it first.

Solo decode: type what you hear. Three points per card, minus one for each hint. Saying it out loud really does help.

Tip: stretch out vowels and run the words together. The phrase usually appears on the third go.
